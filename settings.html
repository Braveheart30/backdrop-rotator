🌱 Eco mode

Uses less energy, mostly by making AI work lighter. AI image generation is by far the most energy-hungry thing this app does.

  • Lighter AI images: the free generator makes smaller images (about 40% fewer pixels to compute), and OpenAI uses its low-quality setting, which needs far less computing than high.
  • Fewer AI images: at most 5 per day.
  • Fewer downloads: auto-rotating searches refresh at most weekly, hold up to 50 photos each, and only refresh while your laptop is plugged in.

The trade-off: AI images look softer, and you get fewer fresh photos.
